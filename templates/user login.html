WELCOME TO THE METHODIST CHURCH NIGERIA, DIOCESE OF APA — YOUTH FELLOWSHIP COMMUNITY!

Grace and peace to you in the name of our Lord and Saviour, Jesus Christ!

This platform is more than just a social feed. It is a place for faith, fellowship, unity, spiritual growth, and meaningful connections.

Here, we encourage one another, share uplifting messages, stay informed about church activities, and grow together in Christ.

“Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.”
— 1 Timothy 4:12 (KJV)
Our Fellowship Values
Faith: Growing together through prayer and the Word of God.
Fellowship: Building stronger relationships across our circuits and local churches.
Unity: Encouraging love, mutual respect, and cooperation among all members.
Spiritual Growth: Sharing testimonies, godly discussions, and uplifting messages.
Service: Using our gifts and talents to serve God and positively impact our generation.
Let us keep this platform respectful, Christ-centred, encouraging, and welcoming to everyone.
One Faith. One Fellowship. One Purpose — Glorifying God and Advancing His Kingdom.
