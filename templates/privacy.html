Platform Privacy Policy

How we handle, protect, and respect your personal information

Transparency Statement: This Privacy Policy accurately reflects the operational data practices of the Convention Management System & Youth Community platform operated by Methodist Church Nigeria, Diocese of Apa.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for or interact with our Youth Community platform, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Registration Details: Full name, chosen username, email address, phone number, and a securely salted bcrypt password hash. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • Church & Fellowship Affiliation: Circuit name, local church society, departmental role, baptismal status, and spiritual interests to assist fellowship coordination.
  • Profile & Media Data: Optional bio, location, occupation, profile picture, cover photo, and social links you choose to add to your user profile.
  • Fellowship Contributions: Posts, comments, reactions, group chat messages, direct messages, and uploaded attachments shared within conversations.
  • Technical Session Data: HTTP session cookies used solely to maintain your signed-in state securely across requests.

2. Why We Collect Information & How It Is Used

We process member data strictly for ministry and church community administration, including:

  • Authenticating member logins and safeguarding accounts from unauthorized access.
  • Facilitating Christian fellowship, Bible study groups, prayer requests, and event notifications.
  • Managing diocesan youth convention registrations, attendance verification, and identity card generation.
  • Enabling church leadership to communicate essential announcements, updates, and programme schedules.

3. Information Visibility & Access

To foster an authentic, trust-centred church community:

  • Registered Fellowship Members: Verified members can view your public profile information (such as name, username, church society, profile photo, and public posts).
  • Direct & Group Conversations: Direct messages are restricted to the conversation participants. Group messages are visible only to members enrolled in that respective group.
  • Administrative Oversight: Authorized diocesan administrators and youth fellowship leadership have administrative access to manage registrations, maintain church records, and ensure adherence to community guidelines.
  • No Third-Party Commercial Sharing: We do NOT sell, rent, license, or share your personal information with commercial advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

4. Data Retention & Storage

Member records, registration files, and community messages are retained in application storage to maintain continuous fellowship records across diocesan convention cycles. Passwords are protected using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. Uploaded documents and media files are stored locally on server storage or designated application volumes.

5. Member Rights & Assistance

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information associated with your profile at any time.
  • Update or correct inaccurate contact, spiritual, or biographical information via the profile edit interface.
  • Request assistance with password recovery or session issues.
  • Request account deactivation or removal by contacting diocesan leadership.

6. Contact Us

If you have inquiries regarding this policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact the Diocesan Youth Fellowship Secretariat, Methodist Church Nigeria, Diocese of Apa.
