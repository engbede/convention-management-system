Youth Community Guidelines

Standards of Christian Conduct, Mutual Respect, and Spiritual Growth

“Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.”

— 1 Timothy 4:12 (KJV)

1. Purpose of Our Fellowship Platform

The Methodist Church Nigeria, Diocese of Apa Youth Fellowship community platform exists to glorify God, advance His Kingdom, foster genuine Christian unity, and support spiritual edification across all circuits and local churches. We are committed to building an encouraging, safe, and edifying space where young people can grow in faith, share godly testimonies, discuss God's Word, and coordinate fellowship programmes.

2. Core Christian Values

  • Faith: We pursue godliness through prayer, biblical study, and wholesome conversation that glorifies our Lord Jesus Christ.
  • Fellowship: We build authentic, loving relationships among youth members across circuits, local societies, and diocesan departments.
  • Unity & Love: We extend grace, forgiveness, mutual respect, and brotherly affection to all brethren, avoiding strife and divisive attitudes.
  • Spiritual Growth: We share testimonies, godly insights, prayer burdens, and constructive contributions that build up the body of Christ.
  • Service & Witness: We deploy our God-given gifts, talents, and youthful energy to serve the church and make a positive impact in our communities.

3. Member Expectations & Responsibilities

All participants within the Youth Fellowship platform are required to adhere to the following standards:

What We Encourage
  • Uplifting biblical scriptures, prayers, and godly devotionals.
  • Constructive fellowship, mentorship, and youth activity updates.
  • Honest testimonies of God's goodness and salvation.
  • Kind, courteous, and respectful communication with every member.
  • Maintaining the privacy and dignity of fellow members and leaders.
Prohibited Activities
  • Profanity, vulgar language, blasphemy, or sexually explicit content.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, defamation, or derogatory remarks.
  • Spamming, commercial solicitation, pyramid schemes, or unapproved promotions.
  • Impersonation of clergy, church officers, or other fellowship members.
  • Sharing unauthorized personal contact information of others.

4. Group Conversations & Channels

Specialized study circles, choir chats, prayer bands, and fellowship groups operate under the supervision of appointed group leaders and moderators. Members must keep discussions relevant to the group's declared purpose and observe administrative guidelines.

5. Content Moderation & Enforcement

To protect the spiritual welfare and harmony of the fellowship, diocesan administrators and designated youth moderators actively review reported content. Violations may result in:

  1. A private advisory or warning from fellowship leadership.
  2. Immediate removal of offending posts, comments, or media attachments.
  3. Temporary suspension of community posting or messaging privileges.
  4. Permanent deactivation of the member account in cases of severe or persistent infractions.

6. Contact & Reporting

If you encounter conduct or content that violates these guidelines, please contact your local youth fellowship executive, circuit coordinator, or diocesan administrator through official church channels.
